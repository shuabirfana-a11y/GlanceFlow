# Post-task questionnaire

Use 1 (strongly disagree) to 5 (strongly agree), plus optional comments.

1. I understood what the HUD was asking me to do.
2. I understood why the system requested clarification or recapture.
3. I understood why the system blocked an action.
4. I felt in control before a calendar side effect.
5. I would trust the system to stop when evidence is insufficient.

This is a project-specific questionnaire, not SUS or another validated standardized scale.
